# Illustrative brand assets

## SBI logo

`frontend/public/brands/sbi.svg` is the State Bank of India logo from [Wikimedia Commons — State Bank of India.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:State_Bank_of_India.svg). Wikimedia Commons describes the graphic as public domain as a simple text/logo graphic and separately notes possible trademark restrictions. The receipt uses it only as an illustration and visibly states that no SBI account is connected and no transaction took place through SBI. The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by SBI.

Review trademark and brand-use requirements before any public marketing or production use.
